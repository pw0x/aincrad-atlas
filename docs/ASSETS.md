# Asset credits · 素材来源

This is a fan-made interpretation of the world created by Reki Kawahara. Rights in *Sword Art Online*, its characters and official artwork belong to their respective holders. The scene notes cite the novels and anime so contributors can trace the setting.

本项目是对川原砾原作世界的同人演绎。《刀剑神域》作品、角色与官方美术的权利归各自权利人。场景说明引用小说与动画，方便参与者核对设定。

Project-authored code is available under MIT. Original artwork, including the journal plates and the project's artistic contributions to scene renders, is available under CC BY 4.0. For a mixed work, follow each contributing asset's licence. [Licensing guide](LICENSING.md) · [MIT](../LICENSE) · [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE-ART.txt)

项目原创代码使用 MIT 许可。原创美术（含手记图版，以及实景截图中本项目创作的部分）使用 CC BY 4.0。复用由多种来源组成的作品时，请一并查阅各项素材的许可。[许可说明](LICENSING.md) · [MIT](../LICENSE) · [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE-ART.txt)
